Available departments:
Accident & Emergency (Chandaria A&E Centre), Cardiology, Oncology/Cancer Centre, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics & Well-Baby, Orthopaedics, ENT, Internal Medicine, Surgery (including open-heart, neurosurgery, transplants), Critical Care/ICU, Radiology, Pathology/Laboratory, Pharmacy, Dental, Physiotherapy, and multiple specialty clinics at Anderson Centre and outpatient sites.

Available clinic services:
Full tertiary referral hospital services including diagnostics, inpatient care, day surgery, maternity, critical care, specialist consultations, travel vaccinations, executive/wellness clinics, and home blood collection (launched 2026). Outpatient centres at Capital Centre (Mombasa Rd), Galleria Mall, Warwick Centre (Gigiri), Rosslyn Riviera, Kiambu Mall, and Southfield Mall.

Laboratory services:
ISO 15189-accredited laboratory offering comprehensive tests (haematology, chemistry, immunology, specialised assays, etc.). Multiple collection points across main hospital and outpatient centres. Home blood collection service available in Nairobi (nominal fee; insurance approval may apply).

Dental services:
Available. Services include consultations, extractions, scaling & polishing, root canal treatment, fillings, teeth whitening, crowns & bridges, paediatric dentistry, orthodontics, and related procedures. Clinic generally operates Monday–Friday (approx. 8:00 am – 5:00 pm); book in advance.

Pharmacy services:
Inpatient and outpatient pharmacies (main hospital has 24/7 coverage). Multiple satellite pharmacies at outpatient centres. Dispenses local and international prescriptions (external prescriptions can be rewritten by hospital doctors if needed). High prescription fill rate, including specialised/imported medicines.

Payment methods:
Cash, M-PESA (Paybill 401400 – use patient/account number as reference), debit/credit cards, bank transfers. Insurance billing where applicable.

Accepted insurance providers:
Works with major local and some international insurers (examples of partners historically/currently engaged include Old Mutual, Britam, AAR, CIC, Madison, First Assurance, Minet, Pacis, Heritage, and others). Coverage was fully restored after temporary disruptions in 2025. Confirm your specific policy with the Insurance Desk (+254 703 082 301, Mon–Fri 8:00 am – 5:00 pm) before treatment. SHA partnerships exist for selected services.

Emergency contact information:

Accessibility information:
Ramps available around the facility. Ample parking. Accessible by public transport (e.g., bus route 46 from Kencom). International patient support and concierge services available.

Other frequently asked questions:

Pre-admission/registration is possible to speed up the process.
Bring government ID, insurance card (if any), doctor’s referral/note, current medications, and personal essentials for admission.
Admission process usually takes 30–60 minutes.
International patients have a dedicated desk for assistance with visas, transfers, interpreters, billing, etc.
Always verify current visiting hours, clinic schedules, and insurance acceptance directly with the hospital, as details can change.
